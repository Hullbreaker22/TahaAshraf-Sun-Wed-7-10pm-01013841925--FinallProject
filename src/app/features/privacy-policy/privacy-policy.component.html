On This Page

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, place an order, or reach out to customer support, we collect details required to deliver your items smoothly.

  • Account profile details including your name, email address, and phone number.
  • Delivery addresses, saved wishlist items, and order history.
  • Session and security tokens used to keep your account protected.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to process orders, coordinate deliveries, send order updates, improve product discovery, and respond to support tickets.

3. Your Choices & Control

You can review or update your account details and saved addresses anytime from your profile, or contact our support team for privacy assistance.
