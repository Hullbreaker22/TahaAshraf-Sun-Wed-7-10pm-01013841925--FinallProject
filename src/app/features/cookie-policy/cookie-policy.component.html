On This Page

1. Why We Use Cookies

Cookies and local storage keep your shopping cart intact, remember your signed-in session, and ensure smooth navigation across pages.

2. Types of Storage Used

  • Essential Session Cookies: Required for login tokens, cart state, and checkout security.
  • Preference Storage: Saves interface settings like theme mode and view preferences.
  • Performance Metrics: Helps identify slow pages and improve store reliability.

3. Managing Preferences

You can clear or block cookies through your browser settings at any time; note that blocking essential cookies may sign you out of your account.
